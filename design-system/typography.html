Typography

Four roles, three faces. Headlines and the editorial accent both use Playfair Display — the accent is expressed through italic style, not a separate font.

Headlines — Playfair Display, 700–900
Uniting makers, teachers, leaders & learners.
Elegant, editorial, timeless. Headlines, section titles, event names.
Editorial accent — Playfair Display Italic
Rooted in belonging.
Reserved for pull quotes, testimonials, invitations, short moments of emphasis. Use sparingly.
Body — DM Sans, 300
Modern, approachable, highly legible. Used for website copy, presentations, marketing materials, and social media.
